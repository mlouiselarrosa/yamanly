Yamanly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Yamanly is a payday-to-payday money planner. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your money details stay on your phone. We do not run a server that collects them.

What we collect

Nothing. Yamanly has no account system, no backend and no analytics. The developer cannot see your name, email, bills, savings, loans, PIN or anything else you enter.

What stays on your phone

  • Your name and email, which you enter when you start. They are only used inside the app to greet you.
  • Your pay dates, bills, loans, tithes, savings, investments, emergency fund, money owed to you, activity history and grocery list.
  • Your PIN, stored as a scrambled value, never as the plain number.

This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. Removing the app or using the reset option inside the app deletes it.

Camera and photos

If you use Groceries (BETA), the app asks to use your camera so you can photograph a price tag. The photo is read on your phone to find the price. It is not uploaded and it is not kept. We never get a copy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone for bills that are due and for people who owe you money. These are created and shown by your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

PDF and backups

When you download your activity as a PDF, or copy a backup text, the file goes only where you choose to send it, such as your files or a chat app. We do not receive it.

Android may back up app data to your own Google account if you have phone backup turned on. That is a setting between you and Google, and you can turn it off in your phone settings.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or send your information to anyone, because we do not have it.

Not financial advice

Yamanly is a planning and tracking tool. It does not connect to your bank, move money, or give financial, tax or legal advice. The numbers you see come from what you enter, so please check them against your real accounts.

Children

Yamanly is made for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Updates that affect how data is handled will be described in the app update notes.
